## How to give me your images

1. Upload the image file(s) into this `uploads/` folder (or just send them in chat).
2. Tell me which slot each image is for, e.g.:
   - *"my-photo.jpg → Carousel Slide 1"*
   - *"manchester-banner.png → Manchester City section"*
3. I will convert/optimize them to the right size & format and swap them in, then rebuild and push.

## Tips
- **Landscape (wide) images** work best for the hero carousel and the Man City section.
- **JPG or PNG** are both fine — I will compress to ~200–300 KB for fast loading.
- Keep the main subject on the **left or center**, because the hero text overlay sits on the left side.
- Avoid images with important detail on the far right edge (it can get cropped on mobile).
